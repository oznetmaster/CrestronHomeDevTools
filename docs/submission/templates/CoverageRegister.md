Operator notes only; this is not an SDK policy, passing evidence or a waiver.

Create one row per official item and enough subordinate rows for its applicable
subconditions and modes. Keep full original wording, including alternatives and
numeric criteria. Do not use the short inventory label as a replacement.

| Official ID and verbatim wording | Capability, modes and applicability rationale | Prior evidence: actual candidate, scope, time, original files | Remaining method, observation and finite repetitions | Timing trigger/response and criterion | Effects, authority consumption and restoration | Disposition and reason | Next action / owner |

Rules:

- Pass means every applicable subcondition in the claimed scope is supported;
  otherwise retain the precise qualification. N/A needs a capability-based reason.
- Keep original test result and current interpretation separate. Later success
  does not erase earlier failures or change their candidate/version.
- Record UI inventory, navigation, command/effect, physical operation and timing
  as separate evidence scopes. Share one sequence across rows when justified.
- Agree finite repetition coverage before acting; do not invent an official count.
- Reuse reviewed applicability and prior evidence until a specific change invalidates
  them. Version mismatch requires scoped impact review, not automatic retesting.
- Whole-row totals must account for every official item exactly once. Preserve
  subcondition detail; do not count both a parent row and its children as items.
- Derive form marks, annex and email claims from this same reviewed register.
- A missing observation route is a preparation blocker; it is not N/A or a pass.
